Why these gaps are hard for incumbents to close
PDI can't compete on transparent pricing — their entire enterprise sales motion is opaque per-site, per-module, per-API quoting. Changing it breaks the model.
Square / Shopify / Clover can't add scan-data or vendor EDI without forking the product into a c-store SKU they don't want to maintain.
Petrosoft / Modisoft sit on legacy stacks where AI is bolted on top of a frozen back-office. Reviewers note dashboards "freeze" and AI sits on broken plumbing.
Ottimate / Lightyear charge per-invoice and split documents in ways that hurt customers economically. Not a c-store fit; never will be.

Shipped, building, and the ask

From thesis to live software in 30 days.

Five wedge features verified, built, and merged. Each maps to a verified Reddit/G2/Capterra owner-pain quote, not a designed-from-scratch idea.

Built & live

  • 01
    Vision-LLM invoice OCR (Groq Llama 4 Scout)
    Replaces Tesseract. One round-trip from photo → structured line items. Falls back gracefully when API is unreachable. Kills the dominant Lightyear / Ottimate complaint pattern.
  • 02
    Tobacco scan-data export — Altria DTP & RJR RMSC
    Weekly file in each program's documented format, with a missing-UPC blocker list. Real rebate dollars Square / Shopify / Clover users leave on the table every week.
  • 03
    Grounded AI assistant — voice + transaction citations
    Five tool-called queries (top products, low stock, margin compression, revenue, vendor performance). Every answer renders the underlying sale-IDs as proof. Incumbents return help-doc links.
  • 04
    Cost-up / retail-flat margin-compression alert
    Auto-fires the moment an invoice is confirmed. Tells the owner the exact retail bump needed to restore margin. Stops the silent margin bleed nobody else catches.
  • 05
    High-shrink cycle-count assistant
    Auto-filters tobacco / lottery / energy SKUs. 5-minute mobile flow saves a snapshot and exposes variance. Replaces the Sunday-night carton count that every operator does by hand.
